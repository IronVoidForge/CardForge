## Rules Text

A squirrel hides 8 acorns. It eats 1. How many acorns are hidden?
Use the fact 8 - 1. Count back 1 from 8, or use the related addition fact 1 + 7 = 8. Write 8 - 1 = 7.
Answer: 7
